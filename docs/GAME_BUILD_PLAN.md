**Where we beat it:**
1. **Art and world.** The reference playground is bare, with grey pillars and a flat sea. Ours gets a full lagoon world with real water, sky, islands, lighting and characters with faces and personality.
2. **Honesty.** Its fake matchmaking screen presents bots as real players, which risks Play's *Deceptive Behavior* policy. Ours labels rivals honestly as AI.
3. **Rating.** It uses skull art and "eliminated" framing (12+). Ours is cartoon slapstick only, targeting an **Everyone** rating.
4. **Depth.** It has only one control verb. We keep the one-touch control but add timing mastery (Perfect / Turbo, §3.2), set-piece variety, bosses, daily content and a collection.

## 3. Game design

### 3.1 Core loop (one race, 40 to 90 s)
1. **Intro flyover** of the course (2.5 s, skippable by tap), with rivals waving on the start pad. Then 3-2-1-**SPLASH!**
2. **Hold anywhere to run, release to stop.** Acceleration and braking are tuned so stopping feels instant (≤ 0.12 s) and starting feels snappy (full speed in 0.25 s). There's no steering: the course carries you.
3. **Read and time.** Every hazard telegraphs itself with a pulsing striped floor zone, an anticipation wind-up, and a sound cue on loopers.
4. **Get bonked:** you get knocked back with a squash, stars and a rubbery *thwap*. If you're knocked off the edge, you **splash** into the lagoon, then respawn at the last **checkpoint flag** after 1.2 s.
5. **Set pieces** break up the timing sections. You **climb** walls, ride **ziplines**, **swing** on ropes, bounce on **jump pads**, cross **monkey bars** and shoot down **water slides**.
6. **Finish:** you run through a confetti arch onto a podium. Your rank pays coins and stars. Top 3 clears the level.

### 3.2 My additions (beyond the reference)
- **Perfect timing → Turbo.** Passing a hazard inside its tightest safe window (a near miss) pops "NICE!" or "PERFECT!" with a 0.25 s slow-mo and fills the **Turbo gauge**. When it's full, your next hold triggers a 3 s sprint with a speed-lines FX. This rewards skill rather than coins.
- **Checkpoint flags** in your colour, which also give a mini-celebration when you pass them.
- **Special levels:**
  - Every **10th level is a boss**, rotating through three: **Rising Tide** (the lagoon floods behind you, so keep moving), **Big Duck Chase** (a giant inflatable duck bounces after the pack) and **Storm Run** (rain, wind gusts and lightning-lit obstacles).
  - Every **5th level (after 10) is a Bonus Coin Rush**: a joyful slide-and-zipline course with no rivals. You can't fail it, and it's the relief beat in the difficulty saw-tooth.
- **Daily Challenge:** a new seeded course every day with a fun modifier (Moon Gravity, Big Heads, Slippery Soap, Mirror Course, Tiny Mode). It pays gems.
- **Time Trial with a ghost:** replay any cleared level against your own best-run ghost for Bronze, Silver or Gold medals.
- **Trophy Road:** podium finishes earn trophies, and a long road of rewards unlocks costumes, hats, trails and dances.
- **Collection:** 30 Dashlings, 24 hats and accessories, 12 trails (bubbles, rainbow, sparkles…) and 10 victory dances, each with a rarity tier.
- **Retention meta:** 7-day login calendar, Lucky Wheel (1 free spin a day), 3 daily missions, 40 achievements and a milestone chest every 10 levels.
- **Pre-race boosters** (coins): **Jet Sneakers** (+20 % speed for the first 8 s), **Bubble Shield** (absorbs one bonk) and **Coin Magnet**. These replace the reference's paid in-race buttons, which clutter the screen.
- **Fair-play pledge:** an ad never interrupts a race. It's written into the ad layer as a hard rule.
- **Accessibility:** danger zones use stripes plus colour (colour-blind safe), a left-handed HUD option, a reduced-motion option (no slow-mo or shake) and adjustable haptics.

### 3.3 Economy
- Coins per race: 1st place 100, 2nd 60, 3rd 40, 4th or lower 15. Coins on the track are worth +1 to +5 each. Turbo PERFECT streaks pay a bonus.
- Gems come from the Daily Challenge, login calendar, missions, achievements and Trophy Road (you can't buy them yet).
- Prices: common Dashling 800, rare 2,500, epic 6,000, legendary 80 gems. Boosters cost 60 to 120 coins. A typical player unlocks something every 3 to 5 levels.
- Every reward is animated: coins fly into the counter with a ticking sound, so players *see* what they earned.

### 3.4 Progression and unlock rules (exactly as you asked)
- Fresh install (debug **and** release): **only Level 1 is unlocked.**
- Clearing a level (top 3) unlocks the next one. Stars: 1st = ★★★, 2nd = ★★, 3rd = ★. 4th or lower means retry, with an encouraging screen ("So close! Try again").
- **Debug build:** tapping **any locked level** opens a **"Watch Ad to Unlock"** dialog. Tapping *Watch Ad* plays a mock full-screen ad (labelled "DEBUG AD", with a 3 s countdown and close button). Then **that level** unlocks and the game offers to play it.
- **Release build:** tapping a locked level shows *"Clear Level N first"* with a button that takes you to Level N. One flag (`AdConfig.rewardedUnlockEnabled`) switches release to the ad flow once you plug in a real SDK. The `AdProvider` interface is ready for AdMob, AppLovin and others.

## 4. 5,000 levels: R&D, metrics and the difficulty curve

### 4.1 How a level is built
A level is a **course spline** (a centreline with banking) assembled from **segments**: straights, curves, ramps, gaps, set pieces and the finish. Each segment can host **hazard slots** with parameters (type, speed, phase, size, side) and **safe pads** (calm zones where you can always stop and wait). The simulation runs in course space (distance along the path plus lateral offset), which makes it deterministic, cheap and exactly testable.

### 4.2 Levels 1 to 10: handcrafted so the player wins

| # | Name | Introduces | Hazards | Rivals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First Splash | Hold to run, release to stop (hand-pointer tutorial). Slow **Bumper Pushers** | 3 | 3 |
| 2 | Swing Time | **Duck Pendulums**, reading the wind-up | 4 | 3 |
| 3 | Spin Cycle | **Noodle Sweeper** (rotating bar) and coins on the track | 4 | 3 |
| 4 | Bounce Bay | **Jump Pads** and first checkpoint flags | 5 | 3 |
| 5 | Slide Away | **Water Slide** and the **PERFECT / Turbo** tutorial | 5 | 3 |
| 6 | Up We Go | **Climb Wall** (hold to climb, coconuts drop from the top) | 5 | 4 |
| 7 | Floaty Crossing | **Moving Floaties** over open water | 6 | 4 |
| 8 | Punch Party | **Boxing-Glove Walls** and a **Turntable** | 6 | 4 |
| 9 | Zip & Swing | **Zipline** and **Rope Swing** | 6 | 4 |
| 10 | Rising Tide | First boss (gentle). Win → chest and a new Dashling | 7 | 4 |

**Win guarantee for 1 to 10:** a *Race Director* rubber-bands rivals so they stay just behind the player. Rivals are capped at 88 % of player speed, they "hesitate" at hazards, and they're never placed so they block you. Hazards run at 0.6 to 0.75× speed with safe windows ≥ 1.4 s. Checkpoints sit before every hazard, and you respawn instantly if you fall. As long as you keep playing, you finish 1st.

### 4.3 Levels 11 to 5,000: generated deterministically and validated
Levels are generated **from a seed** (no 5,000 data files, so the APK stays small) by a grammar of segment templates. **Difficulty score** `D(n)` from 0 to 1:

```
trend(n)  = 0.15 + 0.80 · (1 − e^(−(n−10)/420))       // 0.15 at L11 → 0.30 at L100 → 0.55 at L300 → 0.75 at L600 → 0.87 at L1000 → 0.95 cap
saw(n)    = block shape over each 10 levels: x1 −0.06 (relief), x2…x4 ramp, x5 Bonus Coin Rush, x6…x9 ramp to +0.05, x0 boss +0.08
D(n)      = clamp(trend + saw + seededJitter(±0.02), 0.08, 1.0)
```
The curve rises fast through levels 11 to 300, eases off around 600 to 1,000, then plateaus. It never becomes impossible: after the plateau, variety comes from worlds, modifiers and hazard combinations rather than from raw speed.

**Level metrics derived from D** (interpolated, with a little seeded variety):

| Metric | D 0.15 (≈L11) | D 0.50 (≈L250) | D 0.95 (L2000+) | Hard floor / ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Course length | 160 m | 240 m | 330 m | ≤ 360 m |
| Hazards | 6 | 11 | 17 | ≤ 20 |
| **Minimum safe window** at run speed | 1.30 s | 0.85 s | 0.50 s | **never < 0.45 s** |
| Hazard speed multiplier | 0.75× | 1.0× | 1.35× | ≤ 1.45× |
| Safe pad before each hazard | 3.0 m | 2.2 m | 1.5 m | **≥ 1.4 m always** |
| Combo chains (2 hazards sharing one window) | 0 % | 15 % | 35 % | Triples only after L150 |
| Track width | 4.6 m | 4.0 m | 3.4 m | ≥ 3.2 m |
| Edges with rails | 70 % | 40 % | 15 % | |
| Checkpoint every N hazards | 1 | 2 | 3 | ≤ 45 m apart |
| Rivals | 3 | 4 | 5 | |
| Rival AI skill | 0.40 | 0.65 | 0.90 | |
| Par time (expert run) | 40 s | 60 s | 85 s | 35 to 95 s |

**Hazard unlock schedule.** Each new hazard first appears **alone, slow, behind a safe pad**:
Squish Press L12 · Flip Tiles L15 · Spinning Hoops L18 · Beach-Ball Cannons L22 · Seesaw L26 · Fan Gusts L30 · Pop-up Bollards L34 · Sliding Gates L38 · *2-hazard combos* L40 · Wave Machine L45 · Monkey Bars L50 · Balance Beam L55 · Lazy-River Conveyor L60 · Trampoline Chain L65 · *triple combos* L150.

**Worlds** change every 25 levels and cycle through the 8 worlds. Time of day (morning, noon, sunset, night) rotates on each pass through the cycle, so the same world looks different the next time you see it.

### 4.4 Validation: why every level is "perfect"
Every level passes these checks before it ships. If one fails, the generator re-rolls deterministically with a salt, and the salt table is stored in assets.
1. **Determinism:** the same seed always produces the same level (hash check).
2. **Geometry:** no self-intersection (non-adjacent track sections are ≥ 9 m apart), turn radius ≥ 7 m, slopes ≤ 18°, and the camera never clips scenery.
3. **Stop-and-wait is always possible:** there's a safe pad before every hazard where nothing can reach you. This is the key fairness rule.
4. **Timing fairness:** a phase sweep (simulating every 1/60 s of each hazard's cycle) proves the safe window is ≥ the level's minimum, including combined windows for combo chains.
5. **Readability:** every danger has a telegraph zone. At most 3 hazards are "live" on screen at once (and ≤ 8 animated in view, for performance).
6. **Autopilot runs:** a headless autopilot at skill 0.85 finishes top 3 on every level, and one at skill 0.5 wins levels 1 to 10. The estimated run time must fall inside the par band.
7. A JUnit suite runs all **5,000** levels (the full run happens in WP13), plus a fast 300-level sample for everyday test runs.

## 5. Graphics: the playground gets the most time

### 5.1 Asset pipeline: Blender scripts, not hand modelling
Blender **5.2.3 LTS** is already installed on your Mac, and it runs headless. I'll write Python scripts under `tools/blender/` that **model everything procedurally**: bevels, subdivision-smoothed shapes, seams and quilting on inflatables, and baked ambient occlusion in vertex colours. An exporter writes a compact binary mesh format (`.dmesh`) into `assets/models/`. Everything is deterministic and re-runnable, needs no downloads, and is 100 % original.
- **Dashlings:** one smooth **skinned mesh** (about 3k triangles) with an 18-bone skeleton and automatic weights. Faces are a separate dynamic texture (eyes, brows and mouth change expression: happy, focused, shocked, dizzy, sad). Costumes and hats are attachment meshes on bones.
- **Hazards:** 16 hazard models and 8 set-piece models, each with 2 or 3 world-specific skin variants.
- **Track kit:** straight, curve, ramp, gap edge, rail, pillar, start pad, checkpoint, finish arch, podium.
- **Environment kit:** palm trees, islands with beaches and rocks, lighthouse, boats, buoys, bunting, balloons, gulls, coral, distant volcano, plus per-world props.

### 5.2 Renderer (OpenGL ES 3.0, upgraded from the SnowRace engine)
- **Stylised lighting:** wrap-diffuse sun with a soft 3-band ramp, hemisphere sky/ground ambient, baked AO, a Blinn specular "vinyl gloss" for inflatables and a fresnel rim light. The look is bright, clean and toy-like, with real depth.
- **GPU skinning** for every racer, at up to 6 racers in one pass.
- **Shadows:** a stabilised shadow map fitted to the visible stretch of track. 2048² with soft PCF on High, 1024² on Medium, blob contact shadows on Low.
- **Lagoon water (the star of the show):** Gerstner vertex waves, two scrolling normal maps (generated by us), a sky-reflection fresnel and sun glints. A per-level **shore mask** (a distance field baked once at load) gives turquoise shallows, **foam rings around every pillar and island**, and animated caustics on submerged pillars and sand. Splashes spawn ripple rings and droplets.
- **Sky:** a gradient dome with sun glow, drifting layered cloud billboards, distant island silhouettes with atmospheric fog, and day / sunset / night variants (stars, lanterns and neon at night).
- **Post FX:** MSAA 4× (nearly free on Mali tile GPUs, FXAA fallback), a colour-grading LUT per world, vignette, and half-resolution bloom on High.
- **Particles (GPU-instanced):** splashes, sand and dust puffs, bonk stars, confetti, speed lines, coin sparkles, bubbles and wind streaks.
- **Performance features:** the course is chunked into 20 m sections with frustum culling. Environment props are instanced, colours come from a shared palette atlas (so many objects batch together), and shaders are pre-warmed during the splash. There's an **auto quality tier** from GPU detection, plus **dynamic resolution** (0.7 to 1.0) driven by frame time, locked at a stable 60 fps.

### 5.3 Characters, animation and camera
- **Procedural animation on the skeleton:** run cycle with arm swing and speed lean, idle breathing and blinks, eyes that look at the nearest hazard, a *shocked* face when danger is close, and squash-and-stretch on landings. Plus climb, hang, zipline and swing poses, sliding, a bonk tumble with stars, a flailing splash fall, victory dances (10) and a sad defeat.
- **Secondary motion:** hats, ears and hoodie strings ride on spring chains.
- **Camera:** a spring-damped follow camera with look-ahead that banks on curves and frames each upcoming hazard. It adds an FOV kick on Turbo, a small shake on bonks, slow-mo on PERFECT, a crane shot on set pieces and an orbiting podium cam at the finish.
- **Finish:** a confetti arch, fireworks at night, and the podium with dances and rival reactions.

### 5.4 Eight worlds, each with its own dressing, palette and LUT

| # | World | Track look | Sea | Signature props |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lagoon Park | Inflatable vinyl lanes | Turquoise lagoon | Palms, lifeguard tower, buoys |
| 2 | Candy Carnival | Wafer and gummy tracks | Pink soda sea | Lollipop trees, carousel |
| 3 | Jungle Ruins | Mossy stone | Jungle river with waterfalls | Vines, idols, toucans |
| 4 | Pirate Cove | Plank boardwalks | Sunset bay | Ships, barrels, treasure |
| 5 | Frosty Floe | Ice blocks | Arctic water with ice floes | Penguins, aurora at night |
| 6 | Volcano Isle | Basalt and brass | **Lava** instead of water | Smoke plumes, glowing cracks |
| 7 | Sky Garden | Cloud platforms | A sea of clouds | Balloons, floating islands |

Hazards keep the same rules and shape in every world, but change their skin (for example, the pendulum is a duck, a candy drop, a stone idol, an anchor and so on).

### 5.5 App icon and store art
Rendered in **Blender (Cycles)** from our real 3D assets: a Dashling mid-leap over a noodle sweeper with a lagoon splash. Outputs: adaptive icon (foreground, background and **monochrome** layers) for every density, the in-app logo, a 512² Play icon and a 1024×500 feature graphic.

## 8. Technical architecture

- **Stack:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose (all menus and HUD) and a `GLSurfaceView` running OpenGL ES 3.0 behind a transparent Compose layer. One GL context serves gameplay, the Home diorama, the Shop preview and the onboarding scenes. The simulation runs at a fixed 60 Hz with render interpolation.
- **Reused from your SnowRace project** (ported and renamed): the GL foundation (context, shader framework, shadow map, mesh builder), the audio engine, the atomic save repository, the ad abstraction and the UI component kit. All gameplay, art, levels and the new renderer features are new. This gets us to a solid base faster, so more time goes into the playground.
- **Dependencies added:** `core-splashscreen`, `compose-foundation` and `compose-animation`, `kotlinx-serialization-json` (and its plugin). No game engine, no ad SDK yet, no internet.
- **Persistence:** a single JSON save written atomically (settings, economy, profile, collection) plus a compact binary file for stars and medals across 5,000 levels.

## 12. Honest limits

- No hand-sculpted artist models: the look is high-polish **stylised, script-modelled 3D** (clean bevels, smooth shapes, baked AO, good lighting). The `.dmesh` pipeline accepts artist models later with no code change.
- "5,000 perfect levels" means 10 hand-tuned levels plus 4,990 generated levels, each passing automated solvability, fairness, timing and autopilot checks. They won't each be individually hand-designed.
- The Mali-G57 MP1 is modest. On Low and Medium tiers some effects (bloom, soft shadows, caustics) scale down to keep 60 fps; High-end phones get the full look.
- This is a large build (roughly 22 to 28K lines of code plus asset scripts). It runs as one continuous phase but will take many hours in this session, with device checks after every work package.
