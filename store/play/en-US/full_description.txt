Splash Dash is a bright, bouncy obstacle race on a sunny water park island. Hold to run, let go to stop, and swipe to jump past spinning bars, swinging mallets and flipping tiles. Beat your rivals to the finish line, or splash into the water and try again!

HOW TO PLAY
• Hold anywhere to run, release to stop
• Drag left or right to steer
• Swipe up or tap JUMP to hop over obstacles
• Time your dash through every gap and slip past at the last second for a PERFECT

5,000 OBSTACLE COURSES
Race through 5,000 levels across 8 worlds: Lagoon Park, Candy Carnival, Jungle Ruins, Pirate Cove, Frosty Floe, Volcano Isle, Sky Garden and Neon Pier. Every course is a new mix of obstacles, and the race gets wilder the further you go.

21 CRAZY OBSTACLES
Dodge the Mega Mallet, leap the Spin Bar, squeeze through the Roller Squeeze, fight the Lazy River, outrun the Barrel Roll and hop across the Flip Tiles. Then bounce on trampolines, fly down ziplines, swing on ropes, ride the Floaty Ferry, climb walls, cross monkey bars and zoom down water slides.

BOSS RACES AND CHALLENGES
• Boss races: outrun the Rising Tide, a giant bouncing duck and a howling storm
• Daily Challenge with fun twists: Moon Gravity, Big Heads, Slippery Soap, Tiny Mode and Mirror Course
• Time Trials: beat the clock for bronze, silver and gold medals
• Bump your rivals out of the way, but watch out: the obstacles don't pick sides!

DRESS UP YOUR RACER
Unlock 13 racers and 30 cute Dashlings, 17 hats, 12 trails and 10 victory dances, then show off your style on the podium.

DAILY REWARDS
• A daily gift calendar and a free Lucky Wheel spin every day
• Daily missions, achievements and the Trophy Road
• A treasure chest every 10 levels
• Boosters: Jet Sneakers, Bubble Shield and Coin Magnet

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Quick races you can finish in a minute or two
• A 3D running game with cute cartoon racers and silly splashes
• Fair, skill-based racing: a clean run beats the bots
• Jump in anytime, no account needed

PLAY ANYWHERE
Races work offline, so you can play on the bus, on a plane or anywhere without Wi-Fi. Simple one-touch controls make this running game easy for the whole family to pick up and play.
