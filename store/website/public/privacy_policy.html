Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

💦Introduction

Welcome to Splash Dash: Obstacle Race 3D ("Splash Dash" or the "Game"), a free single-player obstacle racing game developed and published by NGENDEV TECHNOLABS LLP (NG Studio), Rajkot, Gujarat, India ("we", "us" or "our"). In the Game you race cartoon characters through obstacle courses against computer-controlled rivals. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The short version: there is no account and no sign-up, we never ask for your real name, email or phone number, and your progress is saved only on your device. The Game has no ads, nothing is sold for real money and it does not use your advertising ID. To find bugs and improve the Game, it sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase, and it can show you notifications; you can turn both off at any time in the Game's Settings. There is no online play and no chat.

By installing or playing Splash Dash, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

📦1. Information we collect

Information you give us

Only a display name for your racer, which can be any nickname, and the character you pick. They are stored on your device and shown only inside the Game on your device. We never ask for your real name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, location or any other personal information inside the Game.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the Game saves a small amount of data locally on your device only:

  • Your display name and the racer, hat, trail and victory dance you have chosen
  • Your progress: levels unlocked, stars, trophies, Time Trial medals and best times
  • Your coins, gems and boosters, and the racers, Dashlings, hats, trails and dances you have unlocked
  • Your daily gift streak, Lucky Wheel spins, daily missions, Daily Challenge result and Trophy Road rewards
  • Your statistics (such as races, wins and podiums) and achievements
  • Your settings, such as music, sound effects, haptics, graphics, reduced motion, notifications and data sharing

This data is not sent to us, except for the anonymous usage statistics described in Analytics, crash data and notifications. It is not linked to your identity.

Information collected by our partners

Google (through Google Play and Google Firebase) may automatically collect certain technical information, as described in Google Play and Android and Analytics, crash data and notifications below.

📱2. Device permissions

Splash Dash asks Android for these permissions:

  • Vibration, for the small buzz when you bump into an obstacle or cross the finish line. You can turn it off with the Haptics switch in Settings.
  • Internet access and network state, to send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (if you allow it), to receive notifications, and to check for Game updates. Races themselves can be played offline.
  • Notifications, for daily gift reminders and for news about new levels and events. On Android 13 and later the Game asks you first, and you can say no. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Game's Settings or in your Android settings.
  • Run at startup and keep the device awake briefly, used by Android's job scheduler so that a reminder you allowed still arrives after your phone restarts.

The Game does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files, phone calls or messages.

🏁3. Computer rivals, no online play

Every rival you race against is a computer-controlled character run by the Game on your device, not a real person. There is no online play, no chat, no friend list and no way to send messages or share information with other players.

Your display name never leaves your device, but a fun nickname works just as well as a real name. We suggest younger players pick a nickname.

🚫4. No ads, no purchases

Splash Dash does not show advertising and does not include any advertising software. The Game does not read your device's advertising ID, and ad personalisation is switched off in the analytics it uses, so no data is collected for ads.

Nothing in the Game is sold for real money. Coins, gems, boosters, racers, hats, trails and dances are earned only by playing. The Lucky Wheel and treasure chests give only virtual items, which cannot be bought, sold or cashed out.

🔄5. Google Play and Android

Updates

The Game uses Google Play's in-app update service to check whether a newer version is available and, if you agree, to download and install it without leaving the Game. This is carried out by the Google Play Store app on your device under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Apart from the anonymous statistics described below (for example, that an update was offered), we do not receive any information about you from it.

Device backup

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include the Game's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is handled entirely by Google; we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Google Play

When you download the Game, Google Play processes information such as your Google account and device details under Google's Privacy Policy. We may receive anonymous, aggregated statistics from Google Play (for example, the number of installs per country or crash counts) that do not identify you.

📊6. Analytics, crash data and notifications

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To find levels that are too hard and features that need work, the Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records anonymous events, such as screens opened, races started and finished (the level, mode and world, whether you finished, your place, your time, stars, and how often you were bumped or splashed), whether the tutorial was finished, coins and gems earned and spent and on what, rewards from the daily gift, Lucky Wheel, chests and Trophy Road, achievements, your answer to the notification prompt, reminders shown and opened, and update prompts. It also stores your highest level and chosen racer. Each event comes with technical information: an app-instance ID (a random number created for this installation of the Game, not your name or advertising ID), device model, Android version, the Game's language and version, how the Game was installed (for example, from Google Play), approximate location (country or region, worked out from your IP address) and the time of the event.

We do not use these statistics for advertising, and we never link them to your name, email or any account.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the Game crashes or stops responding, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report so we can fix the problem. It contains technical details: what the Game was doing when it crashed (for example, the screen that was open, the level being played, the graphics setting, the graphics level detected for your device and the last few game events), the device model, Android version, memory and storage state, the Game version and an installation ID (a random number for this installation of the Game).

Turning statistics and crash reports off

Both are on by default and can be turned off at any time with the Usage & crash reports switch in the Game's Settings. While it is off, no usage statistics or crash reports are sent.

Google processes this information for us as a service provider under the Firebase terms and Google's Privacy Policy, and keeps it only for a limited time (at most 14 months for usage statistics and 90 days for crash reports). Learn more at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Notifications (reminders and Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you allow notifications, the Game can remind you about your daily gift, your free Lucky Wheel spin or the level you are on. Reminders come 1, 3 and 7 days after you last played, at about the time of day you usually play (between 10:00 and 20:30). Playing again cancels them, so you never get one while you are playing. They are created on your device, and nothing is sent to us to create them.

We may also send news about new levels and events through Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives this installation of the Game a random token so that messages can reach it. Messages go to everyone who plays, never to a named person, and we do not link the token to you.

Turn off both reminders and news with the Notifications switch in the Game's Settings (which also deletes the token), or in your Android settings.

Your device or Google Play may separately offer to share crash reports with developers. That sharing is controlled by your device settings and Google's own privacy policy.

⚙️7. How we use information

The limited information involved in Splash Dash is used only to:

  • Save your progress, coins, gems, unlocked items and settings so you can continue where you left off
  • Find and fix crashes and bugs, and see which levels and features need improving (anonymous statistics and crash reports, if you allow them)
  • Remind you about your daily gift and tell you about new levels and events (if you allow notifications)
  • Offer you updates to the Game
  • Reply to you if you contact us for support

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to build profiles about you.

🤝8. Data sharing

Your progress stays on your device. Information is shared only in these cases:

  • Google Firebase: the anonymous statistics, crash reports and notification token described in Analytics, crash data and notifications are processed by Google on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons: if we are required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, ourselves or others.
  • Business transfers: if the Game is sold or transferred to another developer, this policy (or one at least as protective) will continue to apply.

🗑️9. Data retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Using Settings → Reset progress inside the Game, which erases your levels, stars, coins, gems, unlocked items, statistics, achievements and settings (your display name is kept; change it with Edit name), or
  • Uninstalling Splash Dash, or clearing the app's storage in your device settings, which deletes everything

Anonymous statistics and crash reports are kept by Google Firebase only for the limited time described in section 6. Data collected by Google Play is kept according to Google's own retention policies. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to help you, and then delete it.

🧸10. Children's privacy

Splash Dash is a casual racing game for a general audience. It does not ask anyone, of any age, for personal information: there is no account, no chat and no online play, and the advertising ID is never read.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law of their country. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us (for example, by email), please contact us at store.ngdtechnolab@gmail.com and we will delete it.

🛡️11. Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot normally access. Usage statistics, crash reports and notification data travel to Google over encrypted connections. We work only with reputable partners that use industry-standard safeguards.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but because we hold so little information, there is very little that could ever be exposed.

🎚️12. Your choices

  • Stop sharing statistics and crash reports: turn off Usage & crash reports in the Game's Settings.
  • Turn off notifications: use the Notifications switch in the Game's Settings, or your Android notification settings.
  • Play offline: races work without an internet connection.
  • Change your nickname: use Edit name in Settings at any time.
  • Turn off vibration: use the Haptics switch in the Game's Settings.
  • Delete your data: use Reset progress in Settings, or uninstall the Game.

🌍13. Your rights around the world

You can play Splash Dash from anywhere. Information collected by Google Firebase may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards.

India

We follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You have the right to ask what personal data about you we hold, to have it corrected or erased, and to raise a grievance with us using the contact details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the Game stable and improving it; you can object at any time by turning off Usage & crash reports. Notifications are sent only if you allow them. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion. We do not sell or share personal information, and the Game shows no ads. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Since we do not hold your game data on our servers and the statistics we receive are not linked to your identity, most requests are best handled by the in-game controls above. You are always welcome to contact us for help.

Continuing to play after an update means you accept the revised policy.
