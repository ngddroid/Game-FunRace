1About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Splash Dash: Obstacle Party (the “Game”), made by [STUDIO NAME] (“we”, “us” or “our”), handles information. It applies to this version of the Game for Android (app ID com.ngstudio.funrace).

The short answer: the Game doesn’t collect, send or share any personal information. Everything it needs is kept on your own device.

2What’s saved on your device

To remember your game, the Game keeps a small save file in its own private app storage on your device. It contains:

  • Game progressThe levels you’ve cleared, your stars and trophies, and game stats such as races and wins.
  • Virtual itemsYour coins and gems, plus the costumes and other items you’ve unlocked.
  • SettingsYour choices for options such as music, sound effects, haptics and graphics.
  • Display nameThe name you type in for your racer, and the character you pick.

This information is used only to run the Game on your device, for example to pick up where you left off. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We can’t see it, and we never receive a copy.

Some features, such as daily rewards and challenges, may check your device’s date and time. That check happens on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere.

3Your display name

You can type a display name for your racer. It’s shown only inside the Game on your device. It never leaves your device and isn’t shared with anyone, because the Game has no online features.

Tip for families

A fun nickname works just as well as a real name. We suggest kids pick a nickname rather than using their full name.

4What we don’t collect

We don’t collect, receive, sell, rent or share any personal information through the Game. In particular, the Game does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in;
  • collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts or payment details;
  • access your location, camera, microphone, photos or files;
  • use advertising IDs or other identifiers to recognise you or your device;
  • send information about how you play, or any usage, analytics or crash data, to us or anyone else;
  • connect to the internet. This version doesn’t even request internet access.

If you email us

If you choose to contact us by email, we’ll receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and help you, we don’t share it or use it for marketing, and we delete it once it’s no longer needed for that.

5Permissions

The Game asks Android for just one permission:

Vibration VIBRATELets the Game give haptic feedback, like a little buzz when you bump into an obstacle. You can turn haptics off at any time in Settings.

Vibration is a “normal” Android permission. Android grants it when you install the Game, and it doesn’t give the Game access to any of your information.

The Game doesn’t request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions. Without the internet permission, it can’t connect to the internet at all.

6No ads, analytics or third parties

This version of the Game contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools and no other third-party software (SDKs) that collects data. It shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. We don’t share data with anyone, simply because we don’t have any of your data.

About app stores

The app store you got the Game from (for example, Google Play) handles your download and updates under its own privacy policy, not this one. Stores may give developers general statistics, such as the number of installs, and, only if you’ve agreed to share usage and diagnostics data with the store, crash and performance reports. These don’t tell us who you are, and we use them only to keep the Game running smoothly.

7Android backup

Android has its own backup feature. If you’ve turned it on, Android may include the Game’s save file in your device backup (for example, a backup stored with your Google account), or copy it when you move to a new device, so your progress can be restored.

This is handled by Android and your backup provider, under your device settings and their privacy policy, not by us. We can’t access these backups. You can manage or turn off backups in your device settings, and a backup copy may remain after you uninstall the Game.

8Keeping and deleting your data

Your save stays on your device for as long as the Game is installed, or until you delete it. You’re in control:

  • Reset progress. “Reset progress” in the Game’s Settings (after you confirm) deletes the Game’s saved data from your device: your levels, coins, gems, unlocked items, settings and display name.
  • Uninstall. Uninstalling the Game deletes everything it stored on your device.
  • Clear storage. Clearing the Game’s storage in your Android settings does the same.

Because we never receive your data, there’s nothing on our side to delete, and we can’t recover lost progress for you. Android’s own backup (section 7) may be able to restore it, depending on your settings.

9Children’s privacy

Splash Dash: Obstacle Party is designed for a general audience. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age set by the law where they live). In fact, the Game collects no personal information at all.

There’s no chat, no online play and no way to share information with other people. The rivals you race against are computer-controlled characters, not real players.

Parents and guardians: if you have questions, or believe your child has sent us personal information (for example, by email), please contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we’ll delete it promptly.

10Security

Your save lives in the Game’s private app storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps. Because the Game doesn’t send anything over the internet, there’s no data in transit to intercept, and we don’t hold a copy that could be leaked.

Using a screen lock and keeping your device’s software up to date helps keep your game data safe too.

11Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws (such as the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or state privacy laws in the United States) may give you rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict how it’s used, and to complain to a data protection authority.

Because the Game doesn’t collect or send us any personal data, we don’t hold any information about you from the Game. You stay in control on your device and can delete all of your game data at any time.

If you’ve emailed us, you can ask for a copy of that correspondence, or ask us to correct or delete it. We don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t use it for targeted advertising or profiling.
